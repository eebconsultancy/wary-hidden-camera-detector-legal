Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EEB Consultancy LLC ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Wary: Hidden Camera Detector ("Wary" or the "App").

Wary provides inspection tools that can help you examine your surroundings using network information, your device's camera, and magnetic sensor data.

Wary is designed to perform its inspection functions on your device. The App does not claim that a detected network device, reflection, or magnetic change is proof of a hidden camera or surveillance device.

1. Information We Collect

Wary is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal profile information to use its inspection tools.

2. Local Network Access

Wary may request permission to access your local network so that the Network Scan feature can discover devices and services available on the network to which your device is connected.

Network Scan may process technical information such as:

  • Local IP addresses
  • Network service information
  • Device or service names when available
  • Open network ports checked by the App
  • Protocol and service response information
  • Locally available device-identification information

This information is used to present network inspection results inside the App. Wary does not use the Network Scan feature to determine the physical location or exact distance of devices.

3. Camera Access

Wary may request access to your device's camera for the Camera and Reflection Assist features.

Camera frames are analyzed on your device to help highlight bright or reflective areas that you may want to inspect more closely. Wary does not use this feature to record audio.

The App does not claim that a highlighted reflection confirms the presence of a hidden camera.

4. Magnetic Sensor and Motion Data

The Magnetic Check feature may use motion and magnetic sensor data made available by your device.

These measurements are processed to show changes in the magnetic field relative to a local baseline. Magnetic changes can have many causes and do not identify a specific device or prove that a hidden camera or surveillance device is present.

5. Photos, Videos, and Audio

Wary's inspection features do not require you to upload photos, videos, or audio to EEB Consultancy LLC.

Wary does not use the Camera inspection feature to record audio. Camera access is used to provide the live inspection experience and Reflection Assist functionality.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Wary may offer auto-renewable subscriptions that unlock premium features.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. EEB Consultancy LLC does not directly receive or store your payment card information. Apple may process information related to purchases according to Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

Wary may access subscription entitlement information provided by Apple in order to determine whether premium features should be available on your device.

7. Data Storage

Wary may store app preferences locally on your device, such as appearance, language, feature settings, and onboarding status.

Inspection results and sensor measurements are used to provide the App's functionality. Wary is not designed as a cloud storage service for network scans, camera inspection data, or magnetic measurements.

8. Tracking and Advertising

Wary does not require third-party advertising to provide its core inspection features.

Wary does not use its camera, local network information, or magnetic sensor measurements for advertising purposes.

9. Third-Party Services

Certain functionality may depend on services provided by Apple, including the App Store and StoreKit. Your use of those services is also subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies and terms.

10. Children's Privacy

Wary is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided to us by a child, please contact us.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to design Wary in a way that limits unnecessary collection and transmission of information. However, no software, device, or network environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Your Choices

You can control permissions granted to Wary through your device's iOS Settings. Certain features may not function if the permissions they require are disabled.

You can also manage or cancel your App Store subscription through your Apple Account subscription settings.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Wary, legal requirements, or our practices. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
